# Visual Design System: Obsidian-Emerald Executive

## 1. Visual World & Direction
- **Identity:** **Obsidian-Emerald Executive**
- **Theme Concept:** A high-contrast, editorial executive aesthetic combining deep obsidian (`#071912`), rich corporate forest emeralds (`#0d3b26`, `#1a5c3a`, `#2e8b57`), crisp porcelain canvas surfaces (`#f8fafc`, `#ffffff`), and tailored analytical chart series (Cobalt, Terracotta, Gold, Grape).

## 2. Color Palette & Semantic Tokens
- **Backgrounds:**
  - App Shell Canvas: `#f8fafc` (Warm White)
  - Card & Panel Surfaces: `#ffffff` (Pure White with `#e2e8f0` 1px border)
  - Login / Portal Background: `#071912` (Obsidian Emerald Ambient)
- **Primary Brand Accents:**
  - Deep Forest: `#0d3b26` (Primary Headings & Key Metrics)
  - Forest Mid: `#1a5c3a` (Brand Accents & Primary Buttons)
  - Forest Bright: `#2e8b57` (Interactive Accents & Links)
  - Forest Surface: `#f2f8f4` (Tinted Highlights & Badges)
- **Executive Chart Series:**
  - Series 1: `#1a5c3a` (Forest Mid)
  - Series 2: `#5b8db8` (Cobalt)
  - Series 3: `#d4a843` (Gold)
  - Series 4: `#c05e3c` (Terracotta)
  - Series 5: `#6b5ea8` (Grape)
  - Series 6: `#2e8b57` (Forest Bright)
  - Series 7: `#4a5d73` (Steel)

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Font Family:** Inter / System UI Sans
- **Metric Figures:** Monospace tabular numerals (`font-mono font-extrabold tracking-tight tabular-nums`)
- **Card Headings:** Font weight 700 / 800, leading tight, subtle tracking (-0.02em)
- **Metadata Labels:** Uppercase, tracking wide (0.05em), font size 10–11px, weight 700

## 4. Spacing, Elevation & Radii
- **Grid Gutter:** `16px` (Mobile) / `20px` (Desktop)
- **Card Radius:** `16px` (`rounded-2xl`)
- **Control / Button Radius:** `12px` (`rounded-xl`)
- **Card Border:** `1px solid var(--color-cloud)` (`#e2e8f0`)
- **Card Elevation:** Default `shadow-2xs`, Hover `shadow-md` with `border-steel-light/30`
