# Product Truth & Context

## 1. Product Summary
**Treelife AI Executive Workspace** is an enterprise multi-source business intelligence, document synthesis, and AI-assisted analytics platform. It transforms complex raw operational data (spreadsheets, financial logs, HR tables, PDF documents) into executive dashboards, structured actionable insights, and conversational BI copilot turns.

## 2. Core Personas & Usage Modes
- **Executive Leaders & Founders:** Need immediate executive summaries, high-level KPI trends, strategic warnings/opportunities, and actionable recommendations.
- **Operations & Business Analysts:** Require granular multi-tab cross-domain views, filtered segment metrics, and rapid prompt-based dashboard tailoring.

## 3. Surface Modes
- **Surface Mode: Operate:** The visitor completes tasks, analyzes data, and adjusts dashboard configurations. High data scanability, visual clarity, sub-second interaction feedback, and trustworthy metric citations outrank ornamental distraction.

## 4. Key Architectural Guarantees
- **Zero Dataset Hardcoding:** Platform is 100% upload-driven and agnostic to file structures, sheet names, or column labels.
- **Server-Side Metric Grounding:** All AI insights and chat metrics cite real columns and rows, resolved against raw data.
- **Zero-Lag Tab Navigation:** In-memory stale-while-revalidate client caching for 0ms tab switches.
